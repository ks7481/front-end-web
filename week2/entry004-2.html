Entry 004: Listening

A Voice Memo, 0:14 Seconds, Unlabelled. Route 14, Northbound.

Page 2 of 2

We had the file cleaned up by a friend of the archive who works with audio. He was reluctant, and we don't blame him. When he sent it back, he'd written only: "Listen to it on headphones. Then tell me I'm wrong."

These are our notes, compiled over several listenings. They are not a transcript. We are not sure a transcript is possible.

TimeWhat can be heard
0:00 – 0:03Wind. An empty kind of wind, with nothing to break it up.
0:03 – 0:07Laughter, low in the background. It sounds like two people. One is out of breath. One seems to be laughing at the other for laughing.
0:07 – 0:09A sound like a metal door, or a lid, being shut.
0:09 – 0:11A single voice, very close to the microphone. Words unclear. Our friend hears "one stop early." We hear something shorter.
0:11 – 0:14Wind again. Then the recording ends mid-breath, as if the person who made it had been called away.

We have played this to eleven people. Nine said the laughter sounds happy. Two said it sounds like the kind of laughing you do so that no one hears what you're really doing.

The wind is the strangest thing. There is no engine underneath it. No hum, no rattle, no idle. It was not recorded on a bus. It was not, as far as anyone can tell, recorded in a place that has walls.

We asked the woman who collected the phone, by post, whether she recognised either laugh. We had no reply. But on the following Monday, the recording went missing from the shared drive for exactly fourteen seconds. It came back untouched. We checked twice. The system says it was never gone.

Status
Unclaimed. Retained at the owner's request. Origin unknown.
Note
If you can identify either voice, please leave word at the counter. Do not phone.
