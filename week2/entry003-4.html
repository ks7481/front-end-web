Entry 003: The Door

Set of Keys, Three, on a Ring Shaped Like a Fish. Route 14, Northbound.

Page 4 of 3

This is what was scratched into the door of Locker 14:

Get off one stop before the one you meant.

Underneath, smaller: Not empty. Bring the fish.

We know that first line. It is underlined in blue pen on page 118 of the paperback in Entry 002, the sentence we said we would not print. We didn't. We are not printing it anywhere the archive can see it.

A note from the curator

If you have found this page, you paid attention. Thank you. You are exactly who I have been hoping for.

I should explain why it is here, and why it's like this.

The main pages of this archive have been changing. Not often, and never by much. A word moved, a date shifted, a paragraph I remember writing gone in the morning. I have put things back four times. I have stopped believing it is my own mistake. The recording in Entry 004 went missing from the drive for exactly fourteen seconds, and the system swore it hadn't. Somebody else has a way into this place. I don't know whether they are trying to protect what's here, or to stop it.

So I have started leaving things where the edits don't seem to reach. Inside a link. On a page that isn't counted in the total, and appears in no list and no index. I am not sure how long it will hold. It is the only way I have right now of getting this out. If this page is gone when you come back, that means it did not.

Please don't tell anyone at the counter. I mean that as I have never meant anything, and I can't say more here.

Whatever is in Locker 14, it is not for us to open alone. If you decide to come, come on the 6:40. Sit where you like, except 14B. Bring the fish.

— The Curator
