Entry 005: Tuesday

A Paper Transfer Ticket, Punched, Stamped With a Date That Hasn't Happened Yet. Route 14, Northbound.

After the ticket, we did what we should have done a while ago. We laid the whole archive out on a single table and compared it. The result is below.

Two of five items say 6:40am. A third points to the same Tuesday. A fourth points at a place that has been removed from the map. A fifth is a book in which the name of a destination has been inked out by someone careful, and left showing only two letters, ne. We have not yet worked out what to do with that. Lane, perhaps. Marrow Lane. Or something we're not supposed to guess.

The man in 14B, who held a glove for someone until the next stop. The woman who would not claim a recording, and asked after keys we never told her of. Someone with a fish on a ring and a new key added every few years. Someone with a blue pen. We can't say whether they are four people, or one person, or the same group of people in different weather. We only know they have all been on this route.

The 6:40 northbound will run next Tuesday, as it always does. The driver has agreed to keep seat 14B clear. We will be on it, and we will be carrying the fish.

If you have been meaning to come and collect something, that would be a good week to do it.
