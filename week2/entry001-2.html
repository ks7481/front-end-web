Entry 001: The Card

Left Glove, Grey Wool. Route 14, Northbound.

Page 2 of 2

Three weeks after the line above went onto this page, an envelope turned up in the slot of the counter door. No stamp, no name. It had not come through the post.

Inside was a single index card, written in blue pen. We have reproduced it in full:

Keep it. It was never mine.

I was only holding it for someone until the next stop. There wasn't a next stop.

Please stop asking the seat.

We have gone back over the seat log and found nothing that explains the last line. Nobody at the counter has been asking a seat anything. We checked, and asked around, and the conversation was uncomfortable for everyone.

We did notice something else. The driver's account says the hand wearing the glove kept opening and closing on his knee. Gloves come in pairs, and a man holding one for someone else has, presumably, beenhanded one. Nobody has been able to tell us who was holding the other.

The blue of the ink is a very particular blue. If you have read Entry 002, you may already know why we mention it.

Status
Unclaimed. Confirmed not the property of the man in 14B. Owner unknown.
Note
The card has been filed with the glove. We have not thrown either away.
