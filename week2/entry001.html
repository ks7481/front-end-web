Entry 001: What Happened

Left Glove, Grey Wool. Route 14, Northbound.

Page 1 of 2

The seat log for that Tuesday shows two boardings between Elm and the depot: a woman with a stroller, and a man who paid in coins and didn't sit down until 14B was free.

He got off four stops later, in the rain, without the glove. The driver at the time remembers a phone call — the man had one hand pressed to his ear the whole ride, and the other hand, the one with the glove, kept opening and closing on his knee.

Nobody called about it. No one came to the counter with a description that matched. The ninety days passed the way they always do, quietly, and then it moved from the box under the counter to this one.

We kept it anyway, past policy, for another two weeks after that. Some things you don't immediately let go of.
