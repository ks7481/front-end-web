Same Seat, Same Ride

You take the seat you always take. The ride feels almost identical to any other morning, which after the scramble to get here is oddly comforting. You watch the same streets go by in the same order. You get where you're going on time, but you spend the whole ride checking your phone every thirty seconds anyway, out of habit more than need.
