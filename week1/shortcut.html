You Made It. Barely.

The park path is uneven and you nearly twist your ankle twice, but it saves you almost three minutes. Halfway through, a jogger passes going the other way. She slows down just slightly, and says, "you're late again,". You don't recognize her. You've never seen her before in your life. But there isn't time to think about it, so you don't.

You slide into your seat right as everything is starting, completely out of breath, heart pounding, hair everywhere. Nobody notices you came in late. You made it. The jogger's voice stays with you for the rest of the day.
